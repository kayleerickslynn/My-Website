Behind the Lens & Brush

Hi, I'm Kaylee

Hi, I'm Kaylee.

A photographer and watercolor artist. My motive in both forms of art is best summed up by this quote from Laura Ingalls Wilder:

The true way to live is to enjoy every moment as it passes, and surely it is in the everyday things around us that the beauty of life lies.

My goal is to help you capture the moments before they pass, and to dwell in the beauty of the uniqueness of your life right now.

I specialize in portrait photography, capturing families, friends, and individuals in whatever season of life they are in.

Thank you for being here. I'm excited to work with you!

Every piece of art is printed on gallery-quality archival paper, and every session is guided to feel calm and effortless.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What should we wear?

I recommend soft, neutral tones, natural fabrics (linen, knits, soft cottons), and subtle textures. I provide a complete style guide upon booking.

When is the best time for photos?

Outdoor sessions take place about 90 minutes before sunset for the dreamiest, warmest light. In-home sessions take place late morning when daylight is brightest.
