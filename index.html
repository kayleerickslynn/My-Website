Behind the Lens & Brush

Hi, I'm Kaylee

Hi, I'm Kaylee.

A photographer and watercolor artist. My motive in both forms of art is best summed up by this quote from Laura Ingalls Wilder:

The true way to live is to enjoy every moment as it passes, and surely it is in the everyday things around us that the beauty of life lies.

My goal is to help you capture the moments before they pass, and to dwell in the beauty of the uniqueness of your life right now.

I specialize in portrait photography, capturing families, friends, and individuals in whatever season of life they are in. I paint families too, with an emphasis on family, Christian themes, and nature.

Thank you for being here. I'm excited to work with you!

Client Questions & Details

Photography FAQ's

Everything you need to know about planning and preparing for your family session, locations, wardrobe guidance, and gallery delivery.

I am based in Utah County and primarily serve the surrounding local areas within the county. If you have a specific location in mind outside of my usual radius, please reach out to discuss travel options and any applicable fees.

Mini sessions are brief, typically 20 minutes, and are held on specific dates at set locations. I schedule these with multiple clients coming to the same location back-to-back, and I hold sessions about once a month. Full family packages offer a longer, more relaxed experience (usually 45–60 minutes) at a location of your choice, allowing for a wider variety of candid and posed shots.

Almost all of my learning of basic photography has come from chasing my two toddlers around the home and around playgrounds, switching settings as I’d go to figure out what works best for capturing photos of energetic little ones. I try to embrace the energy of young children and use games, prompts, and a fast shutter speed to capture their genuine expressions while they play and move.

I recommend coordinating colors rather than matching exactly. Earth tones, soft neutrals, and subtle patterns photograph beautifully. Once you book, I will send over a styling guide to help you select outfits that complement the season and our chosen location.

You will receive a link to a private, high-resolution digital gallery within 14 days after your session. From there, you can download your images, share them with family, and order prints.

A non-refundable retainer of 25% of your total package price is required to secure your date. If you need to reschedule due to illness or an emergency, your retainer can be transferred to a new date if requested at least 48 hours in advance. If the weather does not cooperate on the day of an outdoor shoot, we will reschedule at no additional cost.

For the family package, simply fill out the contact form on my website with your preferred dates and package choice. I will be in touch with you shortly after your submission and make sure we solidify all of the details. For the mini session, select the location and time that best fits your schedule and wants, and then proceed to fill out the contact form as prompted.

Art & Print Details

Art FAQ's

Everything you need to know about paper quality, print sizing, custom commissions, framing recommendations, and shipping.

All art reproductions are printed on archival 100% cotton rag fine-art paper using museum-grade pigment inks. This heavy, textured paper offers rich color depth, beautiful tactile finish, and is acid-free to resist fading over decades.

Prints are currently shipped unframed so you can choose a frame and matting that best complements your home decor. Standard print sizes (such as 5x7, 8x10, 11x14, and 16x20) fit easily into ready-made frames from popular retailers.

Custom commissions begin by submitting an inquiry with your concept, preferred dimensions, and reference photos. Once details are confirmed, Kaylee begins hand-crafting your original artwork with progress updates along the way. Finished originals are carefully packaged with care instructions and framing tips.

Standard fine-art prints are printed, packaged, and shipped within 3 to 7 business days. Custom original commissions typically require 3 to 5 weeks from booking to completion depending on the studio queue and medium complexity.

Smaller prints (up to 11x14) are packaged flat with rigid backing boards and protected in archival clear sleeves. Larger prints are carefully wrapped in acid-free tissue paper and shipped in heavy-duty mailing tubes to prevent creasing or edge damage.

Yes! If you have a specific frame or wall dimension that requires custom sizing, please reach out through the contact form or email before placing your order, and we can accommodate custom dimensions whenever possible.

Because each fine-art print is made to order and custom commissions are uniquely personalized, all sales are final. However, if your art arrives damaged during shipping or if there is an issue with your print, please contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos and we will promptly send a replacement.

Photographer & Artist

Welcome!

I am Kaylee, a photographer and artist located in Utah County.

I am married to my best friend who is also a cloud engineer & animal lover, and the mom to Olivia (2) and Leo (1).

I started painting more seriously just after Olivia was born as a creative outlet during nap time. I love painting all kinds of things, but most of my work focuses on Christianity, family themes, and some nature.

I was incredibly excited to have the opportunity to take a photography course after Leo was born. Photography has always been an interest of mine, and I was thrilled to be just as interested or more in the art after going through the course.

When I'm not behind the camera or in the studio, you can find me towing my kids around town in their bike trailer, taking evening walks with my husband, and planning our next getaway to some new destination.

I love what I do, and I love that I can share it with you. Thank you for being here!
